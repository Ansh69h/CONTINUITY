# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participation in CONTINUITY spaces should be respectful, constructive, and focused on the work. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, doxxing, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate when reviewing code and discussing technical disagreements.
- Critique ideas and implementations without attacking people.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's personal information.
- Help keep discussions accessible, relevant, and safe for contributors.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove violating content, restrict participation, or block accounts. To report a conduct concern privately, email [Aryan Dev Singh Mankotia](mailto:aryanmankotia03@gmail.com). For security vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

Maintainers will handle reports discreetly and share details only with people who need them to respond. Enforcement decisions should consider the context, impact, and recurrence of the behavior.
